Relic Description

The “Don’t Check My Bank Account” Wallet™ is a full–length relic wallet cut from genuine cobra skin, with the head mounted dead–center on the front flap. Inside, a black leather core hides a full financial confession: card slots, cash channels, and enough room for passports and paperwork you probably don’t want anyone reading.

This is not a “good habits” accessory. It’s a statement piece for people who know exactly what they’re doing — and do it anyway.

Specs

  • Exterior: genuine cobra skin, head and body panel
  • Color: electric blue & charcoal scale pattern (natural variation per piece)
  • Interior: smooth black leather with SOTP F&F gold stamp
  • Hardware: gold–tone zipper and snap closure
  • Layout: multi–card slots, ID window, bill compartments, zip pocket
  • Construction: handcrafted by Stepani, reinforced stitching at stress points

Fit & Use

  • Designed as a long wallet / travel wallet — fits in coat pockets, bags, and hand–carry.
  • Cobra head is a raised sculptural element; treat it like jewelry, not a phone case.
  • Pairs with the Bad Habits Chain™ and “Do Not Open” Zipper Pulls™ for a full relic system.

Care Notes

Exotic leather ages best when you respect its nature. Keep the wallet dry, out of prolonged sun and away from household cleaners. If it gets damp, blot (don’t rub) with a soft cloth and let it air–dry away from heat. For deep conditioning, use a product formulated for reptile or exotic leather only.

For more detail, see the Care & Craftsmanship page and contact care@sinsofthepast.store before attempting any repair work.

Ethics & Variations

All exotic materials used by Sins of the Past are sourced through partners that follow legal and ethical guidelines. Each cobra wallet will show unique scale patterns, color shifts, and minor textural differences — that individuality is part of the relic. No two are identical, and none are ever restocked in the exact same configuration.
