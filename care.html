Care & Craftsmanship

Natural, ethically sourced materials. High-risk, high-reward silhouettes. Treat them right and they outlive you.

What We Work With

Sins of the Past uses only natural, ethically sourced materials: raw Japanese denim, exotic leathers (python, caiman, cobra), fox and mink fur, full-grain leather, .925 sterling silver, and black diamonds. Every piece is built like a relic, not a trend.

Denim & Mixed Textile Pieces

This applies to: Bad Influence Jeans™, This Seems Illegal Denims™, Pony Play Pants™, Wrong Turn Trousers™, and the “Don’t Ask Me Where I Was” Jacket™.

  • First rule: these are luxury denim pieces, not everyday work jeans. Rotate wear and rest them between outings.
  • Washing (raw denim areas only): turn inside out, cold water, mild detergent, hand wash. Hang dry away from direct sun.
  • Avoid soaking exotic panels: pony hair, python, caiman, or mink trims should not be submerged.
  • No dryers, ever: heat warps leather, shrinks denim, and can crack exotic skins.
  • Storage: hang on wide, padded hangers; avoid overcrowding so tails, heads, and appliqués don’t get crushed.

Exotic Leather & Relic Pieces

This applies to: the “Don’t Check My Bank Account” Wallet™ (cobra), Two-Faced Mink Belt™, exotic panels on denim, and future SOTP leather relics.

  • Keep dry: avoid rain, spills, and steam. If it gets wet, blot with a soft cloth and let it air-dry naturally, away from heat sources.
  • No household cleaners: never use alcohol, baby wipes, or generic leather sprays on exotic skin. If needed, use a conditioner formulated specifically for reptile or exotic leather.
  • Mind the head & raised details: cobra heads and sculpted areas should not be bent or sat on. Treat them like jewelry, not a throw-around wallet.
  • Storage: keep in the SOTP dust bag or a breathable pouch. Lay flat; do not fold exotic heads or tails under pressure.
  • Heat & light: prolonged sun can fade color and dry the scales. Store in a cool, shaded space.

Fox & Mink Fur

This applies to: Back-From-The-Dead Fox Coat™, Bad Decision Aviator™, Text-Me-Back Fox Wrap™, After-Hours Muff™, and the fur side of the Two-Faced Mink Belt™.

  • Professional cleaning only: never wash fur at home. For deep cleaning, use a qualified furrier.
  • If fur gets damp: shake out gently, hang in a cool, ventilated room, and let it air-dry. Do not rub, brush while wet, or apply heat.
  • Storage: hang coats and aviators on broad, padded hangers. Do not store in plastic; use a breathable garment bag.
  • Avoid perfume and hair products: sprays can dry out the pelts over time. Apply before dressing and let them settle.
  • Belts & small goods: store flat in a dust bag, fur side up. Do not clamp under heavy items.

Sterling Silver & Hardware

This applies to: the Bad Habits Chain™, “Do Not Open” Zipper Pulls™, scythe hardware on denim and bags, and any SOTP silver accents.

  • Material: .925 sterling silver with optional black diamond inlay.
  • Daily wear: avoid chlorine, pools, hot tubs, and harsh chemicals. Remove before heavy training or contact with abrasive surfaces.
  • Cleaning: polish gently with a soft jewelry cloth. For deeper tarnish, use a silver-specific polish — never abrasive cleaners or scouring pads.
  • Zipper pulls: when installed on denim or leather, support the pull with your fingers while zipping to avoid torque on the hardware.
  • Storage: keep in anti-tarnish pouches or individual compartments so pieces don’t scratch each other.

Storage, Travel & Repairs

  • Use SOTP dust bags and garment bags for all relics when not in rotation.
  • Do not overpack closets; give tails, heads, and hardware room to breathe.
  • For long-term storage of fur, keep in a cool, dry, dark environment. Avoid attics, garages, or direct sunlight.
  • If a stitch loosens, a scale lifts, or hardware needs adjustment, contact us before attempting any DIY fixes.

Our Promise

Every Sins of the Past piece is built to feel like evidence from a life you probably shouldn’t have lived. In return, we ask that you treat it like a relic, not a throwaway.

If you’re ever unsure how to care for a specific item, email us at care@sinsofthepast.store with a photo of the piece and we’ll advise you directly.
