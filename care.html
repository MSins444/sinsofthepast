What We Work With

Sins of the Past uses natural, ethically sourced materials: raw Japanese denim, exotic leathers, fox and mink fur, full-grain leather, .925 sterling silver, and black diamonds. Every object is built like a relic, not a trend, and arrives with a printed care booklet and verification code.

01

Denim & Mixed Textiles

This applies to Bad Influence Jeans, This Seems Illegal Denims, Pony Play Pants, Wrong Turn Trousers, and the "Don't Ask Me Where I Was" Jacket.

  • First rule: these are luxury denim pieces, not everyday work jeans. Rotate wear and let them rest between outings.
  • Washing: for raw denim areas only, turn inside out, hand wash cold with mild detergent, then hang dry away from direct sun.
  • Avoid soaking exotic panels: pony hair, python, caiman, or mink trims should never be submerged.
  • No dryers: heat warps leather, shrinks denim, and can crack exotic skins.
  • Storage: use broad padded hangers and give tails, heads, and applique room so nothing gets crushed.
02

Exotic Leather & Relics

This applies to the "Don't Check My Bank Account" Wallet, the Two-Faced Mink Belt, exotic denim panels, and future leather relics.

  • Keep dry: avoid rain, spills, and steam. If a piece gets wet, blot gently and let it air-dry naturally away from heat.
  • No household cleaners: skip alcohol, wipes, and generic sprays. Use products made specifically for reptile or exotic leather if needed.
  • Protect sculpted details: raised heads and shaped elements should not be bent, crushed, or sat on.
  • Storage: keep pieces in a dust bag or breathable pouch and never fold heads or tails under pressure.
  • Light and heat: prolonged sun can fade color and dry scales, so store in a cool shaded space.
03

Fox & Mink Fur

This applies to Back-From-The-Dead Fox Coat, Bad Decision Aviator, Text-Me-Back Fox Wrap, After-Hours Muff, and the fur side of the Two-Faced Mink Belt.

  • Professional cleaning only: never wash fur at home. Use a qualified furrier for deep cleaning.
  • If fur gets damp: shake gently, hang in a cool ventilated room, and let it air-dry without rubbing, brushing, or heat.
  • Storage: hang larger pieces on broad padded hangers and use breathable garment bags instead of plastic.
  • Avoid perfume and hair products: apply sprays before dressing and let them settle first.
  • Small goods: store flat in a dust bag with the fur side up and no heavy weight on top.
04

Sterling Silver & Hardware

This applies to the Bad Habits Chain, "Do Not Open" zipper pulls, scythe hardware on denim and bags, and any SOTP silver accents.

  • Material: .925 sterling silver with optional black diamond inlay.
  • Daily wear: avoid chlorine, pools, hot tubs, harsh chemicals, and abrasive contact.
  • Cleaning: polish with a soft jewelry cloth. For deeper tarnish, use silver-specific polish only.
  • Zipper pulls: support the pull with your fingers while zipping to avoid torque on the hardware.
  • Storage: keep pieces in anti-tarnish pouches or separate compartments so they do not scratch each other.
05

Storage, Travel & Repairs

  • Dust bags first: use SOTP dust bags and garment bags whenever a piece is out of rotation.
  • Give objects room: do not overpack closets, luggage, or shelves around tails, heads, hardware, and sculptural details.
  • Long-term storage: keep fur in a cool, dry, dark environment and avoid attics, garages, or direct sunlight.
  • Repairs: if a stitch loosens, a scale lifts, or hardware needs adjustment, contact us before attempting DIY fixes.
06

Booklet & Verification

Each purchased item includes a printed Sins of the Past care booklet tucked into the order as part of the artifact.

The booklet includes your piece-specific handling notes, material guidance, and a verification code that unlocks the next layer of access for the item.

  • Keep the booklet: do not discard it after unboxing. It is part of the piece record.
  • Verification code: each code is unique and tied to the purchased item that shipped with it.
  • Unlock access: use the code to unlock the protected experience, content, or registration layer connected to your item.
  • If lost or damaged: contact us with your order details before sharing the code publicly or attempting unofficial recovery.
